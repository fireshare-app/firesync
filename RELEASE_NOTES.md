## New

- A folder can take each clip's game from the subfolder it is in. Turn on **Each subfolder is a game** on the folder your recorder keeps its per-game folders in — Segra and ShadowPlay both do — and a game you play for the first time needs nothing added in Firesync
- Subfolder names are matched against your library, ignoring case and punctuation. A subfolder spelled differently from its game gets a game of your choosing, or no game, in the folder's settings
- A subfolder that matches nothing holds its clips back as needing attention rather than filing them under the wrong game. They go on their own once you choose a game for it, or add one in Fireshare

## Good to know

- To switch from one watched folder per game to the folder above them, stop watching the per-game folders first. A folder can't be watched with its subfolders while one of them is watched on its own, or its clips would be uploaded twice
